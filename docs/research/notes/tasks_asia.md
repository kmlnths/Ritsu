# Recurring life-admin and health tasks: India, Singapore, Philippines, Malaysia (for Ritsu life tiles)

Scope note: about 20 searches, no full-page fetches (search summaries only), so every item below should be treated as "verify before shipping copy". Where a date is fixed nationally it is marked NATIONAL; where it depends on the user's own date it is marked PERSONAL. LEGAL = official deadline, HABIT = typical practice. Share-of-people figures were almost never found; that is listed as a gap per section.

## India: Car or bike

### Takeaway
The car/bike bundle in India is mostly LEGAL + PERSONAL dates: PUC every 6 months (first year free for new vehicles), yearly insurance, driving licence at 40 / 50 / then every 5 years, and RC renewal at 15 years then every 5 years with a fitness test.

### Cited Findings
- PUC (pollution certificate): new vehicle gets a PUC valid 1 year; after that it must be renewed every 6 months. LEGAL, PERSONAL date. Validity can be shorter if emissions are near the limit. — [ICICI Lombard](https://www.icicilombard.com/motor-insurance/car-insurance/car-guide/blogs/puc-validity-for-new-car); [Tata AIG](https://www.tataaig.com/knowledge-center/car-insurance/puc-validity-for-new-car); [Coverfox](https://www.coverfox.com/car-insurance/articles/puc-validity-for-new-cars/)
- Driving licence (private): valid 20 years or until age 40, whichever first; then renewed for 10 years (to 50); after 50 renewed every 5 years with a medical certificate (Form 1A). LEGAL, PERSONAL date. — [Spinny](https://www.spinny.com/blog/driving-licence-renewal-in-india/); [Cars24](https://www.cars24.com/article/driving-licence-validity-in-india/); [BankBazaar](https://www.bankbazaar.com/driving-licence/senior-citizen-dl-rules.html)
- Possible change: reports that DL validity may be extended to age 50. Unconfirmed, treat as pending. — [AutoPunditz](https://www.autopunditz.com/post/driving-licence-validity-age-50-online-vehicle-transfer)
- RC (registration) for private vehicles: valid 15 years, then renewable every 5 years; a fitness certificate is required at renewal. LEGAL, PERSONAL date (every few years). — [Zurich Kotak](https://www.zurichkotak.com/knowledge-center/car-insurance/vehicle-rc-renewal); [Tata AIG](https://www.tataaig.com/knowledge-center/car-insurance/renew-car-registration-after-15-years)
- PUC and insurance are linked in practice (insurers ask about PUC). — [Zurich Kotak](https://www.zurichkotak.com/knowledge-center/car-insurance/is-puc-certificate-mandatory-for-vehicle-insurance)

### Inferences
- Suggested bundle: "PUC check" (every 6 months), "Vehicle insurance renewal" (yearly, personal date), "Service" (habit, see gaps), "Licence renewal" (at 40, 50, then 5 yearly), "RC renewal" (15 years, then 5 yearly). Most of these are rare enough that the app should ask for the date once, not guess.
- Third-party motor insurance is compulsory under the Motor Vehicles Act (well known; not re-verified this session, see gaps).

### Gaps
- Not verified this session: third-party insurance mandate citation, typical service intervals (commonly quoted as every 10,000 km or yearly for cars, 3,000 to 5,000 km for bikes, but no source fetched), FASTag KYC/recharge rules (the 2024 "KYV" and annual pass changes were not checked), Delhi-NCR 10/15-year age bans.
- No data found on share of Indian adults owning a car or two-wheeler.

## India: My home

### Takeaway
Home tasks are mostly HABIT + PERSONAL: rent agreement every 11 months (legal-ish, personal date), LPG refill every few weeks, monthly bills. Only a few have firm rules.

### Cited Findings
- Rent agreements are usually 11 months because leases of 12 months or more must be compulsorily registered (stamp duty and registration cost roughly 1.5% to 5% of annual rent by state). So renewal every 11 months is a real recurring task for renters. LEGAL-adjacent, PERSONAL date. — [RentOk](https://rentok.com/blogs/market-trends/why-is-a-rental-agreement-made-for-11-months-only); [eDrafter](https://www.edrafter.in/11-month-rent-agreement-rules/)
- LPG booking: a minimum gap between delivery and next booking applies. Sources conflict: a 21-day gap rule was reported; later reports say the gap is 25 days (one says "cut from 45 to 25 days"), with rule changes listed for October 2026. Treat the exact number as unstable. — [Deshabhimani (21 days)](https://english.deshabhimani.com/deshabhimani-english-/national-76192/india-lpg-cylinder-booking-21-day-gap-rule-55307); [Angel One (21 to 25 days)](https://www.angelone.in/news/economy/lpg-booking-gap-increased-to-25-days-from-21-days-government-introduces-new-rule); [Sunday Guardian (Oct 2026 rules)](https://sundayguardianlive.com/india/lpg-cylinder-booking-new-rules-october-2026-check-25-day-refill-gap-aadhaar-authentication-subsidy-delivery-rules-lpg-prices-kyc-status-more-changes-from-october-1-check-key-deadlines-295523/)

### Inferences
- LPG should be a user-set interval ("every N weeks"), not a fixed number, because both household usage and the minimum gap vary.
- Monthly: electricity, water, society maintenance, broadband/DTH, domestic help salary. Quarterly or twice a year: water purifier service, AC service (before summer), pest control. Yearly: property tax. These are sensible defaults but unsourced.

### Gaps
- No sources fetched for society maintenance cycles, water purifier / AC / pest control intervals, property tax due dates (they vary by municipality, e.g. many cities have an early-payment rebate window around April to June, unverified), or broadband/DTH recharge cycles.
- "9 subsidised refills" (PMUY) not confirmed.

## India: Money

### Takeaway
India has clear NATIONAL money dates: advance tax 15 Jun / 15 Sep / 15 Dec / 15 Mar, ITR 31 July (ITR-1/2) or 31 August (ITR-3/4, new for AY 2026-27 per sources). Everything else (EMIs, SIPs, card bills, premiums) is PERSONAL monthly or yearly.

### Cited Findings
- Advance tax FY 2026-27: 15 June (15%), 15 Sept (45%), 15 Dec (75%), 15 March (100%). Presumptive-tax freelancers/small businesses (44AD/44ADA) can pay all by 15 March. LEGAL, NATIONAL. — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/advance-tax); [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/indias-advance-tax-due-dates-tax-year-2026-27-45178.html/)
- ITR for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27), non-audit: 31 July 2026 for ITR-1 and ITR-2; 31 August 2026 for ITR-3 and ITR-4. Belated return by 31 December. LEGAL, NATIONAL, yearly. — [ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/due-date-tax-filing); [CalcGuru](https://calcguru.in/itr-due-date-ay-2026-27/); [SmartTaxCalc](https://smarttaxcalc.in/tax-calendar/)
- Note: India's new Income-tax Act 2025 renames "assessment year" to "tax year" from April 2026 (India Briefing refers to "tax year 2026-27"). Copy should say "tax filing" not "AY". — [India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/indias-advance-tax-due-dates-tax-year-2026-27-45178.html/)

### Inferences
- Advance tax only matters to people with non-salary income (freelancers, business, big interest/capital gains), so it belongs in "Work or business" or behind a question, not in the default Money bundle.
- Default Money bundle for a salaried Indian: "File tax return (July)", "Credit card bill" (monthly, personal), "EMI / SIP" (monthly, personal), "Insurance premiums" (yearly, personal), "Tax-saving investments before 31 March" (habit, old regime only).

### Gaps
- Not verified: GST return dates for small businesses (GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B monthly or quarterly under QRMP), PPF minimum Rs 500 yearly deposit by 31 March, KYC re-verification cycles, Aadhaar-PAN link (a one-off, not recurring).
- No share data for how many Indians file ITR or hold credit cards.

## India: Health

### Takeaway
Kids' vaccines follow a NATIONAL schedule keyed to the child's birth date (personal date, fixed intervals). Adult health checks in India have no official recurring schedule that I found.

### Cited Findings
- Universal Immunisation Programme (UIP): at birth (BCG, OPV-0, Hep B), 6 weeks, 10 weeks, 14 weeks (OPV, Pentavalent, Rotavirus, fIPV, PCV), 9 to 12 months (MR-1), 16 to 24 months (MR booster), 5 years (DPT booster); Td at 10 and 16 years. Covers 12 diseases. — [Wikipedia UIP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_Immunisation_Programme); [NHM Himachal](https://nhm.hp.gov.in/immmunization); [Pharmapedia](https://thepharmapedia.com/national-immunization-schedule-universal-immunization-programme-uip-india/)
- An Indian-made Td vaccine was launched 21 February 2026 (per search summary of a PIB release; not fetched). — [PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2241066&reg=3&lang=2)

### Inferences
- Adult defaults should be HABIT and soft: "Yearly health check", "Medicine refill" (monthly, personal), "BP / sugar check" (user-set). Per project rules, never prescribe; say "ask your doctor how often".

### Gaps
- No official Indian adult screening frequency found (NPCDCS/NP-NCD population screening for 30+ exists but frequency not verified). Share of adults with diabetes/BP not gathered.

## India: Parents

### Takeaway
One strong NATIONAL task: pensioners' life certificate every November (from 1 October if 80+).

### Cited Findings
- Jeevan Pramaan / life certificate: all central government pensioners submit 1 to 30 November; those 80+ can submit from 1 October. Missing it can stop pension. LEGAL, NATIONAL, yearly. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/personal-finance/pensioners-aged-80-above-can-submit-annual-life-certificate-in-october-124101400253_1.html); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeevan_Pramaan); [Kotak Neo](https://www.kotakneo.com/news/personal-finance/jeevan-pramaan-life-certificate-october-window-for-80-plus-pensioners/)

### Inferences
- Parents bundle: "Life certificate (November)" only if the parent gets a pension; "Parents' medicine refill" (monthly); "Parents' check-up" (user-set); "Parents' insurance renewal" (yearly, personal).

### Gaps
- Share of Indian elderly on pension not found. State and bank pensioners may have different windows (not verified).

## Singapore

### Takeaway
Singapore has tidy NATIONAL dates (tax filing 18 April) and PERSONAL cycles (road tax 6 or 12 months, dog licence yearly, NS ICT yearly, Healthier SG check-in yearly).

### Cited Findings
- Car: road tax renewable for 6 or 12 months; off-peak / weekend cars only 12-monthly. LEGAL, PERSONAL date. — [LTA OneMotoring](https://onemotoring.lta.gov.sg/content/onemotoring/home/owning/ongoing-car-costs/road-tax.html); [Moneysmart](https://blog.moneysmart.sg/transportation/lta-road-tax-renewal/)
- Money: individuals must e-file income tax by 18 April each year (filing opens 1 March). LEGAL, NATIONAL. — [IRAS e-Filing](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/understanding-my-income-tax-filing/e-filing-your-income-tax-return); [IRAS Tax Season 2026](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/understanding-my-income-tax-filing/tax-season-2026---all-you-need-to-know)
- Health: Healthier SG enrollees do a Health Plan check-in with their family doctor; recommended yearly for healthy people, in-person at least once every 3 years if no chronic condition. Screening intervals: BMI yearly; diabetes, BP, cholesterol every 3 years; colorectal (FIT) every 2 years; cervical (HPV, 30+) every 5 years. Fully subsidised check-ins. — [Healthier SG benefits](https://www.healthiersg.gov.sg/enrolment/benefits/); [HealthHub screening](https://www.healthhub.sg/programmes/healthiersg-screening); [MOH](https://www.moh.gov.sg/newsroom/one-time-1-500-healthier-sg-healthpoints-benefit-to-encourage-regular-health-plan-check-ins-/)
- Work (men): NSmen do about 10 yearly In-Camp Training cycles over a ~10-year ORNS cycle; IPPT is required once per 12-month window starting from the birthday after ORD. LEGAL, PERSONAL (birthday-based) date. — [OneNS](https://www.ns.gov.sg/web/profiles/nsman/orns); [MHA NS Portal](https://ns.mha.gov.sg/nsp/app/ns-and-you/ippt-and-ns-fit)
- Pets: dog licence renewed yearly (or longer terms) via PALS. Rabies vaccination is encouraged, not compulsory, for pets in Singapore (sources in search summary; rabies page not fetched). — [Pawrenthood](https://www.pawrenthood.sg/dog-license-singapore-rules-fees-and-how-to-apply/); [AVS Rabies](https://avs.nparks.gov.sg/about-us/what-we-do/animal-health/rabies/)

### Inferences
- Healthier SG screening intervals are ideal for a Health tile because they are official and calm ("every 3 years").
- IPPT/ICT fits "Work" or a separate question ("Are you an NSman?"); applies only to men after full-time NS.

### Gaps
- Not verified: HDB service and conservancy charges (monthly, town council), COE 10-year expiry and 5/10-year renewal, CPF MediSave top-up timing, vehicle inspection for cars 3+ years (every 2 years, then yearly at 10+ years; not verified). Cat licensing changes (a cat licensing scheme was planned; not checked).

## Philippines

### Takeaway
Car registration month is NATIONAL-by-plate (last digit of plate = month). Tax and social contributions are monthly (employer-handled for employees) or quarterly/annual for the self-employed.

### Cited Findings
- LTO registration renewal month by last plate digit: 1 = January ... 9 = September, 0 = October; second-to-last digit sets the week. LEGAL, yearly, date derived from plate. — [Top Gear PH](https://www.topgear.com.ph/features/feature-articles/motor-vehicle-registration-schedule-ph-a2619-20220410); [Globe](https://www.globe.com.ph/blog/lto-motor-vehicle-car-registration-renewal)
- BIR annual income tax return (1701/1701A) normally due 15 April; for 2025 income it was extended to 15 May 2026. Self-employed also file quarterly 1701Q: 15 May, 15 August, 15 November. LEGAL, NATIONAL. — [Taxumo extension](https://www.taxumo.com/blog/bir-extends-2025-annual-income-tax-filing-deadline-to-may-15-2026/); [Taxumo 1701Q](https://taxumo.com/blog/know-your-1701q-deadline-2026-a-practical-guide-for-freelancers-and-business-owners/)
- 2026 contribution rates: SSS 15% of monthly salary credit (10% employer, 5% employee); PhilHealth 5% (split); Pag-IBIG 2% each, ceiling PHP 10,000. SSS due last day of following month; PhilHealth and Pag-IBIG windows depend on ID/name. Monthly. — [Kami Workforce](https://kamiworkforce.com/ph/blog/sss-philhealth-pagibig-contribution-tables-2026/); [SweldoPH](https://sweldoph.com/guides/payroll-remittance-deadlines-2026)

### Inferences
- For employees these are paid by the employer, so they are NOT user tasks. They become user tasks for voluntary/self-employed/OFW members (monthly). Ask "Do you pay SSS / PhilHealth / Pag-IBIG yourself?"
- Car bundle: "LTO registration (your month from plate)", "Insurance (CTPL is needed for registration)", "Emission test before registration" (commonly required; unverified).

### Gaps
- Not verified: barangay clearance (usually a one-off per need, likely not recurring), community tax certificate (cedula, yearly, January), Meralco billing (monthly), driver's licence validity (5 or 10 years), pet rabies vaccination rules (RA 9482 requires yearly dog vaccination and registration; not fetched).

## Malaysia

### Takeaway
Car tasks bundle neatly (insurance then road tax, yearly, same date; Puspakom for older cars). Tax filing 30 April / 15 May (e-filing). Zakat is a Muslim yearly task with a tax rebate.

### Cited Findings
- Road tax (LKM) cannot be renewed without valid insurance; renewals are yearly (also possible 6-monthly for some; not verified). PUSPAKOM inspection needed before renewal if the car is 15+ years (Peninsular) or 20+ years (Sabah/Sarawak), per a 2026 guide. Road tax expired over 3 years needs a special inspection. — [JPJ](https://www.jpj.gov.my/en/motor-vehicle-license-renewal-guide-lkm/); [Paultan 2026](https://paultan.org/2026/06/22/how-to-renew-your-car-insurance-and-road-tax-in-malaysia-2026/); [Bjak](https://bjak.my/blog/car-insurance/grace-period-car-insurance-road-tax-malaysia-real-rules)
- Caution: the "15 years Puspakom" rule comes from secondary sources; I did not confirm it on JPJ/Puspakom. Mark uncertain.
- Income tax (Form BE, salaried): manual by 30 April, e-filing by 15 May. For YA 2025 LHDN also granted a 15-day extension to 15 May. Filing opens 1 March. LEGAL, NATIONAL. — [Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2550924); [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/04/29/2025-tax-return-form-deadline-extended-to-may-15); [RinggitPlus](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/tax/you-have-until-may-15-to-file-your-tax-return.html)
- Zakat fitrah: every Muslim, yearly, during Ramadan before Eid prayers (moves each year). Zakat pendapatan (income) 2.5%, pay by 31 December to get a 100% tax rebate that year. — [RinggitPlus](https://ringgitplus.com/en/blog/banking/everything-you-need-to-know-about-zakat-in-malaysia.html); [Ajobthing](https://www.ajobthing.com/resources/blog/zakat-pendapatan-in-malaysia-who-must-pay-how-to-calculate-and-claim-100-tax-rebate)
- EPF i-Saraan: voluntary contributions for self-employed under 60, government incentive 20% up to RM500 per year (lifetime RM5,000). 1.3 million contributors as of Sept 2026. — [KWSP](https://www.kwsp.gov.my/en/member/savings/i-saraan); [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2026/09/16/epfs-i-saraan-draws-13-million-contributors-rm101bil-in-savings)
- Pets: dog licence is annual per dog from local councils (DBKL RM60/year from April 2024), rabies vaccination proof required; rabies booster yearly. (Secondary sources.) — [Malaysia4u](https://malaysia4u.com/pets-guide); [TrustyTails](https://www.trustytails.vet/blog/dog-vaccination-schedule-malaysia-2026)

### Inferences
- Malaysia car bundle: "Insurance + road tax" as ONE yearly item (they are renewed together), plus "Puspakom" for old cars.
- Zakat fits a Money or Faith item only if the user opts in; Ramadan date moves, so needs a calendar source.
- "Top up EPF before year end" is a good gentle yearly item for self-employed users.

### Gaps
- Not verified: Malaysian driving licence renewal (1 to 10 years, user chooses), TNB/water bills (monthly), Muslim-majority share (roughly 60%+, not sourced this session).

## Cross-country patterns (for the tile design)

### Takeaway
Across all four countries, most recurring tasks are PERSONAL dates on fixed intervals (6 months, 1 year, 5 years); only tax filing and a few items (India life certificate, advance tax) are NATIONAL. So a tile should mostly ask "when is yours due?" and fill the interval itself.

### Cited Findings
- National tax dates: India 31 July/31 Aug ([ClearTax](https://cleartax.in/s/due-date-tax-filing)), Singapore 18 April ([IRAS](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/basics-of-individual-income-tax/understanding-my-income-tax-filing/e-filing-your-income-tax-return)), Philippines 15 April, extended to 15 May in 2026 ([Taxumo](https://www.taxumo.com/blog/bir-extends-2025-annual-income-tax-filing-deadline-to-may-15-2026/)), Malaysia 30 April / 15 May e-file ([Bernama](https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2550924)).
- Recent changes to flag: India ITR-3/4 due 31 August for AY 2026-27; India LPG booking gap changes (2026); PH and MY both extended 2026 tax deadlines to 15 May (one-off extensions; default back to 15 April / 30 April + 15 May unless extended again).

### Inferences
- Deadlines get extended often (PH and MY both did in 2026). Store the normal date but let the user edit, and avoid "overdue" copy (matches Ritsu rules).
- Employer-handled items (PH contributions, India TDS, MY EPF, SG CPF) should not appear as user tasks by default.

### Gaps
- Share-of-people figures (car ownership, pet ownership, pension, tax filers) were not found in this session for any country.
- Travel and Studies tiles were not researched (passport validity: India 10 years adult, SG 10 years, PH 10 years, MY 5 years; all unverified here).
