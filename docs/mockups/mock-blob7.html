Rules it follows

It is never unhappy with you. A missed habit, a skipped day or a low score never changes its face. The Sad one is only sympathy, and the Serious one only guards things that cannot be undone.
It speaks rarely. Bubbles appear for a few seconds at real moments, never as a nag, and the Side eye at most once a week.
It stays out of the way. It never covers a button, and it never shows on your reports or the share card.
