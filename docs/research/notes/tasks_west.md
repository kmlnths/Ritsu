# Recurring life-admin and health tasks: US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland (by Ritsu life tile)

Legend used in lists: **Freq** = how often. **Kind** = LEGAL (official deadline or legal duty) or HABIT (good practice). **Date** = NATIONAL (same date for everyone) or PERSONAL (depends on the user's own start date, birthday, purchase date and so on). **Share** = rough share of people it applies to, where known.

Source note: items marked [verified 2026] were checked against a live search or page in this session (30 Sep 2026). Items marked [known, not re-fetched] cite the official page that holds the rule, but the page was not re-opened in this session; the rule is long-standing and unlikely to have changed, but treat the exact figure as "check before shipping".

## Car or bike

### Takeaway
Every country has a yearly (or near-yearly) registration/tax renewal plus insurance renewal, all on a PERSONAL date. Roadworthiness tests differ a lot and several changed in 2025 and 2026 (Texas and New Hampshire dropped safety inspections; New Zealand is moving to longer WoF intervals from 1 Nov 2026), so the app should ask for the date rather than assume a rule.

### Cited Findings
**United States**
- Vehicle registration renewal: yearly or every 2 years depending on state; LEGAL; PERSONAL date (usually tied to purchase or birth month). Periodic safety inspection states as of 2026 include Hawaii, Maine, Massachusetts, New York, North Carolina, Pennsylvania, Vermont, Virginia, West Virginia; New Hampshire ends annual safety and emissions inspections on 31 Jan 2026 [verified 2026] — [Insurify, state inspection requirements 2026](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/vehicle/state-vehicle-inspection/); [Wikipedia: Vehicle inspection in the US](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_inspection_in_the_United_States)
- Texas eliminated safety inspections for most passenger vehicles from 1 Jan 2025 but kept emissions testing in certain counties (Houston, Dallas areas); Louisiana safety inspections effectively end for personal vehicles 30 Jun 2026 [verified 2026, secondary source; summary contradicted itself by also listing Texas as an annual-safety state, so treat Texas as emissions-only] — [Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/vehicle/state-vehicle-inspection/); [TCEQ emissions overview](https://www.tceq.texas.gov/airquality/mobilesource/vim/overview.html)
- Statewide emissions testing: Connecticut, Delaware, Massachusetts, New Jersey, Rhode Island; urban-area-only emissions in e.g. Arizona (Phoenix, Tucson), Colorado (Denver), Georgia (Atlanta), Illinois (Chicago), Texas metros. About 14 states require no regular safety inspection [verified 2026, secondary] — [Insurify](https://insurify.com/car-insurance/vehicle/state-vehicle-inspection/); [Vermont Legislative Research Service, Nov 2025 (PDF)](https://www.uvm.edu/d10-files/documents/2025-11/State-Motor-Vehicle-Safety-Inspections-and-Emissions-Testing.pdf)
- Driver's licence renewal: varies by state, commonly every 4 to 8 years; LEGAL; PERSONAL (often birthday) [known, not re-fetched] — [USA.gov driver's licenses](https://www.usa.gov/state-motor-vehicle-services)

**United Kingdom**
- MOT: first at 3 years old (4 in Northern Ireland), then every year; LEGAL; PERSONAL [known, not re-fetched] — [GOV.UK Getting an MOT](https://www.gov.uk/getting-an-mot)
- Vehicle tax (VED): yearly, 6-monthly or monthly direct debit; LEGAL; PERSONAL [known] — [GOV.UK Vehicle tax](https://www.gov.uk/vehicle-tax)
- Photocard driving licence: renew photo every 10 years; at 70 and every 3 years after, renew entitlement; LEGAL; PERSONAL [known] — [GOV.UK Renew driving licence](https://www.gov.uk/renew-driving-licence); [GOV.UK Renew at 70](https://www.gov.uk/renew-driving-licence-at-70)
- Car insurance renewal yearly; LEGAL to hold, HABIT to compare at renewal; PERSONAL [known] — [MoneySavingExpert car insurance](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/insurance/cheap-car-insurance/)

**Canada**
- Registration/plate sticker renewal: provincial; e.g. Ontario 1 or 2 years (Ontario removed sticker fees in 2022 but renewal is still required) [known, not re-fetched] — [Ontario: renew licence plate sticker](https://www.ontario.ca/page/renew-licence-plate-sticker)
- Insurance: yearly (public insurers in BC, SK, MB; private elsewhere); LEGAL; PERSONAL. Driver's licence: typically every 5 years; LEGAL; PERSONAL (birthday) [known] — [ICBC renew](https://www.icbc.com/driver-licensing/renew-replace-update)
- No routine national periodic safety inspection for private cars in most provinces (Quebec and Nova Scotia have some requirements) [uncertain; not verified this session]

**Australia**
- Registration ("rego"): 3, 6 or 12 months; LEGAL; PERSONAL — [Service NSW renew registration](https://www.nsw.gov.au/driving-boating-and-transport/driving-nsw/vehicle-registration/renewing-vehicle-registration/how-to-renew-vehicle-registration) [verified 2026]
- NSW: CTP green slip bought before each rego renewal; pink slip (safety check) mandatory for most vehicles over 5 years old, used within 6 months of issue [verified 2026] — [Allianz NSW rego explainer](https://www.allianz.com.au/car-insurance/articles/vehicle-registration-nsw.html); [iSelect pink/blue/green slips](https://www.iselect.com.au/car-insurance/pink-and-blue-slips/)
- Other states: CTP is bundled into rego in VIC, QLD, WA, TAS, NT; roadworthy certificates usually only at sale or re-registration, not yearly [known, not re-fetched; mark uncertain per state]

**New Zealand**
- Warrant of Fitness: CHANGING. From 1 Nov 2026 new light vehicles get a 4-year first WoF, 4 to 14-year-old vehicles move to 2-yearly (phased: registered on/after 1 Nov 2019 eligible from 1 Nov 2026; registered 1 Nov 2013 to 31 Oct 2019 from 1 Nov 2027), over-14-year vehicles annual; the 6-monthly WoF is scrapped. Previously: annual for most, 6-monthly for older cars; LEGAL; PERSONAL [verified 2026] — [NZTA: changes from 1 Nov 2026](https://vehicleinspection.nzta.govt.nz/news/changes-to-light-vehicle-inspections-from-1-november-2026); [1News Apr 2026](https://www.1news.co.nz/2026/04/17/warrant-of-fitness-changes-what-you-need-to-know/)
- Vehicle licence ("rego"): 1 to 12 months chosen by owner; LEGAL; PERSONAL. Road user charges for diesel and (from 1 Apr 2024) EVs, bought in 1,000 km blocks [known] — [NZTA vehicle licensing](https://www.nzta.govt.nz/vehicles/licensing-rego/)

**Ireland**
- NCT: first test at 4 years; every 2 years for cars 4 to 10 years old; yearly 10 to 30 years; every 2 years 30 to 39 years (non-commercial); LEGAL; PERSONAL [verified 2026] — [Citizens Information: NCT](https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/travel-and-recreation/motoring/motor-tax-and-insurance/national-car-test/)
- Motor tax: disc for 3, 6 or 12 months; LEGAL; PERSONAL [verified 2026] — [Citizens Information: Motor tax](https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/travel-and-recreation/motoring/motor-tax-and-insurance/motor-tax-rates/)
- Driving licence renewal usually every 10 years [known] — [NDLS](https://www.ndls.ie/)

### Inferences
- Across all six countries the universal core bundle is: registration/tax renewal, insurance renewal, roadworthiness test (where applicable), licence renewal, and a HABIT service (typically yearly or by mileage). All are PERSONAL dates, so the tile should ask "when is it due?" once per item rather than preset dates.
- For the US and Australia the test item should be optional per state; for NZ the app needs a "check your WoF sticker" date since the interval changes mid-transition.

### Gaps
- Could not parse the Vermont legislative PDF (binary); US state list rests on secondary sources.
- Canada provincial inspection rules and Australian non-NSW roadworthy rules not verified this session.
- No reliable share-of-population figure found; car ownership is high in all six (US highest), but not researched here.

## My home

### Takeaway
Home bundles mix a few LEGAL yearly national dates (UK council tax year from April, TV Licence) with many PERSONAL contract dates (insurance, broadband, energy fixes, lease) and HABITS (smoke alarm test, boiler/furnace service, gutters, HVAC filter).

### Cited Findings
- UK TV Licence: yearly, £180 from 1 Apr 2026 (up £5.50); LEGAL if watching live TV or BBC iPlayer; PERSONAL renewal date [verified 2026] — [MoneySavingExpert Feb 2026](https://www.moneysavingexpert.com/news/2026/02/tv-licence-fee-rise/); [TV Licensing FAQ](https://www.tvlicensing.co.uk/faqs/FAQ23)
- UK council tax: annual bill from April, usually paid in 10 (or 12) monthly instalments; LEGAL; NATIONAL year, PERSONAL instalment day [known] — [GOV.UK Council Tax](https://www.gov.uk/council-tax)
- UK landlords must get a gas safety check every 12 months (tenants may need to give access) — LEGAL for landlords; PERSONAL [known] — [HSE gas safety landlords](https://www.hse.gov.uk/gas/landlords/)
- Smoke alarms: UK fire services advise testing monthly [known] — [GOV.UK fire safety in the home](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/fire-safety-in-the-home); US: test monthly, replace alarms at 10 years [known] — [US Fire Administration / NFPA](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/prevent-fires/smoke-alarms/)
- Broadband/phone contract end: UK providers must send an "end of contract" notification 10 to 40 days before the minimum term ends (Ofcom rule since 2020) — HABIT to switch; PERSONAL [known] — [Ofcom end-of-contract notifications](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/phones-and-broadband/bills-and-charges/end-of-contract-notifications)
- UK energy price cap changes quarterly (1 Jan, 1 Apr, 1 Jul, 1 Oct); NATIONAL; HABIT to check a fixed deal [known] — [Ofgem energy price cap](https://www.ofgem.gov.uk/energy-price-cap)
- US property tax: yearly or semi-annual, dates set by county; often paid via mortgage escrow; LEGAL; LOCAL date [known, general] — [USA.gov](https://www.usa.gov/)
- Ireland: Local Property Tax yearly (due by direct debit or lump sum; valuation date 1 Nov 2025 for 2026 to 2030) [known, not re-fetched] — [Revenue LPT](https://www.revenue.ie/en/property/local-property-tax/index.aspx)

### Inferences
- Useful HABIT items with typical cadence (no single legal source): HVAC/furnace filter every 1 to 3 months (US/Canada); boiler service yearly (UK/IE); gutters twice a year; home insurance renewal yearly; lease renewal yearly (PERSONAL); HOA dues monthly/quarterly (US, PERSONAL). These are well-known practices but I did not fetch a source for each; mark as HABIT, not LEGAL.

### Gaps
- No sourced US property tax dates (varies by 3,000+ counties): the app should just ask.
- Australia/NZ council rates (quarterly instalments are common) not verified this session.
- Share of renters vs owners not researched.

## Money

### Takeaway
Yearly tax deadlines are NATIONAL and fixed per country; self-employed people get extra quarterly or twice-yearly dates. The UK has a big 2026 change: Making Tax Digital for Income Tax started 6 Apr 2026 with quarterly updates for sole traders and landlords over £50,000.

### Cited Findings
- US: federal individual return due 15 April; self-employed estimated tax quarterly (15 Apr, 15 Jun, 15 Sep, 15 Jan) [known, not re-fetched] — [IRS estimated taxes](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/estimated-taxes)
- US: free credit reports from each bureau (now weekly, permanently) [known] — [FTC free credit reports](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/free-credit-reports)
- US: ACA Marketplace open enrollment 1 Nov to 15 Jan in most states; employer benefits open enrollment usually each autumn (employer-set) [known] — [HealthCare.gov dates](https://www.healthcare.gov/quick-guide/dates-and-deadlines/)
- UK: Self Assessment online return and balancing payment 31 Jan; payments on account 31 Jan and 31 Jul; paper return 31 Oct; register by 5 Oct [known] — [GOV.UK Self Assessment deadlines](https://www.gov.uk/self-assessment-tax-returns/deadlines)
- UK Making Tax Digital for Income Tax: started 6 Apr 2026 for sole traders/landlords with qualifying income over £50,000; threshold falls to £30,000 (2027) and £20,000 (2028); quarterly updates, first due 7 Aug 2026 (quarter 6 Apr to 5 Jul); first final declaration 31 Jan 2028 [verified 2026] — [HMRC MTD quarterly updates](https://makingtaxdigital.campaign.gov.uk/quarterly-updates/); [LITRG](https://www.litrg.org.uk/tax-nic/making-tax-digital-income-tax/when-does-making-tax-digital-start-me)
- Canada: file and pay by 30 April; self-employed file by 15 June but still pay by 30 April; interest from 1 May; instalments 15 Mar, 15 Jun, 15 Sep, 15 Dec if tax owing over $3,000 (current or either of previous 2 years) [verified 2026] — [Canada.ca 2026 deadlines self-employed](https://www.canada.ca/en/services/taxes/resources-for-small-and-medium-businesses/2026-tax-deadlines-canadian-businesses-self-employed-individuals.html)
- Australia: self-lodged individual tax return due 31 October (later if using a registered tax agent); tax year ends 30 June [known, not re-fetched] — [ATO lodge your tax return](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/your-tax-return/how-to-lodge-your-tax-return/lodge-your-tax-return-by-31-october)
- New Zealand: IR3 returns due 7 July (to 31 March next year with a tax agent extension); many salary earners get automatic assessment instead [verified 2026] — [IRD 7 July](https://www.ird.govt.nz/pages/campaigns/7-july); [IRD timelines](https://www.ird.govt.nz/income-tax/income-tax-for-individuals/what-happens-at-the-end-of-the-tax-year/timelines-at-the-end-of-the-tax-year)
- Ireland: Pay and File 31 Oct 2026; ROS extension to 18 Nov 2026 only if both paying and filing through ROS [verified 2026] — [Chartered Accountants Ireland](https://www.charteredaccountants.ie/News/2026-ros-pay-and-file-extension-date-announced); [Revenue eBrief 034/26](https://www.revenue.ie/en/tax-professionals/ebrief/2026/no-0342026.aspx)

### Inferences
- The subscriptions review and credit check are HABITS (suggest yearly or quarterly), not deadlines.
- Only a minority file Self Assessment in the UK or IR3 in NZ; for employees the tax item could be off by default with a "Self-employed?" toggle.

### Gaps
- Share of self-employed per country not researched.
- US state income tax deadlines differ; most follow 15 April but not verified.

## Health

### Takeaway
Screening intervals are set nationally and differ by country and age; dental and eye checks are HABIT intervals set by the clinician. England changed cervical screening to every 5 years for ages 25 to 64 from 1 Jul 2025; the US moved breast screening to every 2 years from age 40 (2024). Ritsu must present these as "ask your doctor" starting points, not advice.

### Cited Findings
- England cervical screening: from 1 Jul 2025, ages 25 to 64 invited every 5 years (was every 3 years for 25 to 49) unless higher risk; Scotland (2020) and Wales (2022) changed earlier; LEGAL/official programme, PERSONAL date (invitation letter) [verified 2026] — [Cancer Research UK Jun 2025](https://news.cancerresearchuk.org/2025/06/10/nhs-england-changes-cervical-screening-to-every-5-years/); [Eve Appeal](https://eveappeal.org.uk/news/cervical-screening-interval/)
- US breast: USPSTF recommends mammogram every 2 years for women 40 to 74 (updated 2024 from age 50) [verified 2026] — [USPSTF breast cancer screening](https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/breast-cancer-screening)
- US colorectal: screen ages 45 to 75 (45 to 49 grade B, 50 to 75 grade A); interval depends on test (e.g. stool test yearly, colonoscopy every 10 years) [verified 2026 for ages; intervals known] — [PMC on USPSTF colorectal](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC11450516/)
- UK dental recall: NICE guideline CG19 says recall interval 3 to 24 months for adults based on risk (up to 12 months for under-18s) [known, not re-fetched] — [NICE CG19](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg19)
- UK eye test: NHS suggests every 2 years (more often for some) [known] — [NHS eye tests](https://www.nhs.uk/nhs-services/opticians/free-nhs-eye-tests-and-eyewear-vouchers/)
- UK breast screening every 3 years for women 50 to 71; bowel screening home test every 2 years 50 to 74 in England (age extended to 50 by 2025) [known, not re-fetched] — [NHS breast screening](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/breast-screening-mammogram/); [NHS bowel screening](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/bowel-cancer-screening/)
- Flu vaccine: yearly in autumn, free for eligible groups in all six countries (e.g. US CDC recommends everyone 6 months+) [known] — [CDC flu vaccine](https://www.cdc.gov/flu/vaccines/index.html)
- Australia: National Bowel Cancer Screening kit every 2 years ages 45 to 74 (45 to 49 opt-in since 2024); cervical every 5 years 25 to 74; BreastScreen every 2 years 50 to 74 [known, not re-fetched] — [Australian Govt bowel screening](https://www.health.gov.au/our-work/national-bowel-cancer-screening-program)

### Inferences
- Prescription refills are PERSONAL (monthly typical) HABIT items; they must not count toward the day per Ritsu rules.
- Dental: US typical 6 months (insurance usually covers 2 cleanings a year) vs UK NICE 3 to 24 months; default suggestion "every 6 months, change if your dentist says otherwise".

### Gaps
- Canada, NZ and Ireland screening intervals not verified this session (provincial variation in Canada).
- Share of adults due for each screening not computed.

## Kids

### Takeaway
Kids tasks are mostly PERSONAL (vaccination schedule by birth date, dentist visits) plus NATIONAL school-term dates set by state/province/local authority.

### Cited Findings
- US childhood immunization schedule is published yearly by CDC (note 2025 to 2026 federal changes to vaccine advisory process make this area politically unsettled) [known; recent changes not verified this session] — [CDC child schedule](https://www.cdc.gov/vaccines/hcp/imz-schedules/child-adolescent-age.html)
- UK: NHS routine vaccination schedule by age [known] — [NHS vaccinations](https://www.nhs.uk/vaccinations/nhs-vaccinations-and-when-to-have-them/)

### Inferences
- School term dates vary by local authority (UK), district (US), state (AU); app should let users type them, not preset.
- Activity fees and school lunch top-ups are HABIT, monthly or termly.

### Gaps
- Not researched in depth: child dental interval per country, school fee cycles, US back-to-school forms. Treat as open.

## Parents

### Takeaway
Only a few official windows; the rest is caregiving HABITS (medicine refills, appointments, bill checks).

### Cited Findings
- US Medicare: Initial Enrollment Period is 7 months around 65th birthday (3 before, birth month, 3 after); Open Enrollment 15 Oct to 7 Dec yearly; Medicare Advantage open enrollment 1 Jan to 31 Mar [known, not re-fetched] — [Medicare.gov when you can join](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/sign-up/when-does-medicare-coverage-start)
- UK: driving licence renewal every 3 years from 70 (useful for parents) [known] — [GOV.UK renew at 70](https://www.gov.uk/renew-driving-licence-at-70)

### Gaps
- Power of attorney has no legal review interval in these countries as far as found; "review yearly" would be a HABIT with no official source. Not verified.

## Pets

### Takeaway
Annual vet check and vaccine boosters are HABITS; a few places have LEGAL licences (Ireland dog licence yearly or lifetime; many US cities/counties).

### Cited Findings
- Ireland: dog licence required from 4 months old; individual €20 yearly or lifetime €140; €150 on-the-spot fine; microchip required since 31 Mar 2016 [verified 2026] — [Citizens Information: dog licences](https://www.citizensinformation.ie/en/environment/pets-and-wildlife/control-of-dogs/); [Mayo County Council](https://www.mayo.ie/dog-warden/licenses)
- US: dog licences and rabies vaccination (1 or 3 year vaccine) are set by state/county; e.g. rabies vaccination is legally required in most states [known, general; not verified per state]

### Inferences
- Typical HABIT cadence: flea/tick monthly, worming every 1 to 3 months, annual vet check. Vets differ; no single official source fetched.
- NZ: dog registration yearly with the local council (typically due by 1 July) [known, not verified]; worth adding to NZ Pets.

### Gaps
- UK and Australia council pet registration rules (AU councils require yearly or lifetime registration) not verified.

## Travel

### Takeaway
Passports renew roughly every 10 years for adults and 5 for children; electronic travel permits (ESTA, UK ETA) are 2-year renewals and prices rose in 2025 and 2026.

### Cited Findings
- US ESTA: $40 since 30 Sep 2025, valid 2 years (or until passport expires) [verified 2026] — [Wego / Rowan summaries](https://withrowan.co.uk/guides/eta-cost-validity)
- UK ETA: £20 from 8 Apr 2026 (was £16), valid 2 years or until passport expires [verified 2026] — [Home Office ETA factsheet Apr 2026](https://homeofficemedia.blog.gov.uk/electronic-travel-authorisation-eta-factsheet-april-2026/)
- Passport validity: US 10 years adult, 5 child under 16 [known] — [travel.state.gov](https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/passports.html); UK 10 years adult, 5 child [known] — [GOV.UK renew passport](https://www.gov.uk/renew-adult-passport); Ireland 10 adult, 5 child (3 for under 3) [known]; Canada 5 or 10 years adult choice [known] — [Canada.ca passports](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/canadian-passports.html); Australia and NZ 10 adult, 5 child [known]
- Canada eTA: valid up to 5 years [known, not re-fetched] — [Canada eTA](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/visit-canada/eta.html)

### Inferences
- Many countries need 6 months of passport validity to enter; a "renew 9 months before expiry" nudge is a sensible HABIT.
- Annual multi-trip travel insurance renewal is PERSONAL yearly.

### Gaps
- EU ETIAS start date not researched (possibly relevant for UK/US/Canada/AU/NZ travellers).

## Work or business

### Takeaway
For the self-employed, tax instalments are the main NATIONAL fixed dates (US quarterly, Canada quarterly, UK MTD quarterly plus 31 Jan/31 Jul, NZ provisional tax). Professional licences and CPD are PERSONAL and vary by profession.

### Cited Findings
- See Money section for US quarterly estimates, Canada instalments and UK MTD quarterly updates (due 7 Aug, 7 Nov, 7 Feb, 7 May pattern) [MTD first date verified] — [HMRC MTD](https://makingtaxdigital.campaign.gov.uk/quarterly-updates/)
- NZ provisional tax and GST have fixed dates by filing frequency [verified 2026, secondary] — [nztax.tools deadlines](https://nztax.tools/tax-deadlines/)

### Gaps
- Profession licence/CPD cycles (e.g. nursing, teaching) not researched; would need a per-profession list.

## Studies

### Takeaway
Student-loan repayments and US FAFSA are the recurring official items.

### Cited Findings
- US FAFSA: filed every academic year; the form opens 1 October (returned to 1 Oct from the 2026/27 cycle after the 2024/25 delay) [known; 2026 opening not verified] — [StudentAid.gov](https://studentaid.gov/h/apply-for-aid/fafsa)
- UK student loans: repaid automatically through PAYE monthly above a threshold; self-employed via Self Assessment [known] — [GOV.UK repaying student loan](https://www.gov.uk/repaying-your-student-loan)

### Gaps
- Canada, Australia (HELP via tax return), NZ (via IRD) and Ireland student repayment and term fee cycles not verified this session.
