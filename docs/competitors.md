# Competitors and the name

Written 2026-09-29. Sources: ritsuapp.com and its App Store page, read that day. No reviews, revenue or user numbers were found, and none are guessed here.

## The other Ritsu (ritsuapp.com)

| | Other Ritsu | Our Ritsu |
|---|---|---|
| Tagline | "Plan the week. Run it clean." | Health and to-dos in one calm place |
| For | Professionals and knowledge workers | Anyone's whole day: medicine, body, habits, tasks |
| Core idea | Plan, run and review a week of work and projects | Fair scoring; logs and medicine never punish |
| Where | iPhone, Android, web, already in both stores | Web app installed to the home screen |
| Price | Free, then $4.99, $7.99, $13.99 a month | Not decided |
| Standout | AI note-to-plan (top paid tier), Gantt, calendar import, analytics | Sentence typing that works offline, Home card with lights, blob |
| Feel | Calm, "structure without noise" | Calm, warm, never nagging |

They help people work; we help people live. Little overlap in users, same calm feeling.

## Ideas worth borrowing

1. **A weekly view**: a calm Sunday "Plan the week" card on Home. Skipping is safe, nothing goes red.
2. **Calendar import** (.ics or .csv): a small link in setup step 3; items are sorted into cards and groups and the user unticks what they do not want.
3. **One line that says it all**: ours could be "Your whole day, one calm place." Wording is open.

Not to copy: Gantt charts, time tracking, AI as a paid upgrade (they pull toward work; our rule-based typing is free and offline).

## The naming problem

- Same name, same store category, and theirs is already listed.
- Risk is low while Ritsu is a free website; it grows at the native app step and if we charge money.
- Before a paid launch or store listing: pick a name that can be fully ours, and get a proper trademark search (not legal advice).
- Still open: a new name that keeps the meaning of 律 (rhythm, order).
