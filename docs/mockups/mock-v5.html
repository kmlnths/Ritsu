Ritsu v5 mock

The new way to add things: a library of ready-made bundles, one habit form, gear tied to habits, and a weekly fasting plan. Everything is tappable. All numbers are sample data.

1. What are you adding?

One question instead of ten words. The library sits on top, because most people add something common.

2. The library

Pick a habit and its gear comes along, already set up. Untick anything you do not want. Search finds gear too, so "toothbrush" finds Brush teeth.

3. New habit

Three types, like Streak, in plain words. Steps and gear are questions, off until you say yes.

4. A habit with gear

Gear counts days, uses, or both, whichever comes first. Uses come from your ticks on the habit. Tap "Log a floss" to watch them count.

5. Fasting plan

Set your week once. Ritsu picks today's target for you, and adjusts around what you actually do. It never raises a target by itself.

Stop a fast whenever it feels wrong, and a short fast still counts. If you take medication with food, check with your doctor first.

Mock only. Sample data, nothing is saved.
